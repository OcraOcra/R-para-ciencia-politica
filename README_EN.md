# Applied Statistics for Political Science (Spanish Translation)

This is a **Spanish translation and adaptation** of J.K. Corder's 
[*Applied Statistics for Political Science*](https://jkcorder.github.io/), 
a textbook on applied statistics for political science using R.

## What's different?

- **Full translation to Spanish** of all chapters and examples
- **Localized examples** using data from Costa Rica's CIEP survey (n=969, November 2020)
- **Practical exercises** with solutions for instructors
- **Step-by-step procedures** for statistical tests (z-test, t-test, ANOVA, Chi-square)
- **Simulation-based learning** to understand regression models
